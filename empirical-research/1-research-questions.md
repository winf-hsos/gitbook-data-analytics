# 1 Research Questions

A research question serves as the foundation of a research study, guiding the inquiry and directing the focus toward specific objectives. A good research question is clear, focused, and researchable, offering a path to explore complexities within a subject. It should be neither too broad nor too narrow, allowing for comprehensive analysis while remaining manageable within the scope of the study. A well-framed research question stimulates critical thinking, encourages investigation, and ultimately leads to significant contributions to the field. It aligns with the research objectives, assists in determining the research design, and helps in identifying relevant literature, methods, and interventions.
